# Product Vision: Medonivo Health OS

## Brand and Identity
* **Name**: Medonivo
* **Extension**: Medonivo Health OS
* **Tagline**: The Intelligent Healthcare Platform
* **Promise**: One Patient. One Journey. Every Branch.

## The Core Problem
Healthcare delivery is historically siloed. Patients navigate disjointed channels (separate booking sites, clinical records databases, pharmacy receipts, and lab reports). Hospitals run disjointed operational systems that do not sync in real-time, resulting in long waiting queues, duplicate patient profiles, data leakages, and untracked financial leakages (due bills).

## The Medonivo Solution
Medonivo bridges these boundaries by combining:
1. **Patient Experience Platform**: Unified profiles, family-care authorizations, digital bookings, and secure report downloads.
2. **Hospital Information System**: Queue management, reception walk-ins, practitioner scheduling.
3. **Diagnostic Management System**: Sample tracking, laboratory result entries, and pathologist approvals.
4. **Healthcare Billing Platform**: Multi-branch invoice matching, cashier shift audits, and partial-payment allocation.
5. **Patient Membership Platform (CarePass)**: Subscription benefits seamlessly checked and applied automatically.

## Primary Target Markets
* **Initial Launch**: Bangladesh (BDT, Asia/Dhaka time zone, English and Bangla localization).
* **Future Scaling**: Configurable locale variables to support expansion without core logic changes.
